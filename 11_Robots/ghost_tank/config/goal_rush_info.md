NOTE: putting goal rush down is coupled with opening clamp
1. Put goal rush arm down (clamp is now open)
2. Put goal rush arm up (clamp closes, but arm is still down due to weight of goal)
3. Move backwards to pull goal into area
4. Put goal rush arm down (to open clamp and let go of goal)
5. Move backwards to release goal
6. Pull goal rush arm up (clamp will close and arm goes up due to no goal weight)
